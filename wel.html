Dear Damola,

I'm not sure when it happened, but somewhere between your first smile and the second one, I lost my footing. It's your smile, mostly. It starts small, like you're trying to keep it to yourself, and then it takes over your whole face and I forget what I was saying. I've caught myself replaying it at random moments, in line for coffee, on the train, right before I fall asleep. And you're beautiful, in a way that makes me a little nervous. Not just the way you look, though that's plenty, but the way the room seems warmer when you're in it. I don't know where this is going, but I know I want to find out. Maybe over dinner? Maybe over a very long walk where I try (and fail) to act casual?

Yours, hopelessly smitten,
Abdulbasit (Mr B)
